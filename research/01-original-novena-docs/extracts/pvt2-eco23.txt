<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="ECO23:_improve_regulator_stability_on_cold_power-on">ECO23: improve regulator stability on cold power-on</span></h2>
<p>ECO13 changes the inductor. This has introduced some instability on cold power-on. Oscilloscope waveforms show a lot of noise on the 5V line after jamming the power cable in, and the 5V regulator subsequently shuts itself off. 
</p><p>Adding another 22uF cap on the 5V output filtering (C23N DNP-&gt;populated) eliminates this noise and the system seems to perform stably across multiple cold power-on events.
</p><p>Issued to factory as ECO-0002 against PVT2 rev A on October 15 2014. 
</p>
