EDP adapter EVT

From Studio Kousagi Wiki
Jump to: navigation, search

This is the production eDP adapter using the IT6251 adapter chip. EVT build was 10 units.

ECO 1: L15P is missing

L15P is missing due to a BOM error.

EVT PVT Notes
L15P BLM18KG121 (DNP) L15P BLM18KG121

ECO 2: C17P / L10P swapped due to assembly error

C17P is inserted into L10P's slot.

Silkscreen is reworked to make it more clear which component is which. No BOM change, but please take note for assembly and ask if anything is unclear.

ECO 3: R10P/R11P are swapped

R10P and R11P were swapped in the BOM.

EVT PVT Notes
R11P 10k, 1% R11P 20k, 1%
R10P 20k, 1% R10P 10k, 1%
